Rune Cast: cast the stones, read the three that land closest

Drop nine Elder Futhark runes on the cloth. Stones that land face down stay unread, as in the old method. The three face-up stones nearest the centre are read for you in one of three AI voices.


How Rune Cast works

  1. Choose a voice

    Pick Tally, Verra or Hollis. Each is an AI writing style, clearly labelled, with its own way of reading the same stones.

  2. Ask one question

    Keep it open and specific. How and what questions work better than yes or no.

  3. Cast the stones

    Nine runes tumble onto the cloth. Face-down stones are left unread; the three face-up stones nearest the centre are read.

  4. Read the reflection

    The AI writes a short reflection stone by stone, then ties them together and ends with one practical step.

Three AI voices, one cast

The same three stones can be read in very different ways. Tally separates what is symbolic from what you can check or do. Verra turns the cast into a short northern saga. Hollis treats each rune as a question worth asking yourself. All three are AI writing styles, not people, and none of them can know what will happen.

Because the stones are drawn at random, casting again gives a different picture. Try the same question twice, or the same stones in a different voice, and notice which themes keep returning.

Rune Cast questions

Is this a real rune reading?

No. Rune Cast is an automated tool. The stones are drawn at random in your browser, and an AI writes a reflection in the style of the voice you pick. It is not a live session, and the voices are not real people.

How are the runes chosen?

Nine stones are drawn at random from the 24 runes of the Elder Futhark and dropped on the cloth. Some land face up and some face down. Following the old way of casting, only face-up stones are read, and only the three that lie closest to the centre.

Can a rune cast predict the future?

No. Runes are symbols. A cast can give you themes to think about and questions to ask yourself, but it cannot know what will happen and it should not replace practical advice.

Are the three AI voices different people?

They are three writing styles. Tally is plain and measured, Verra tells the cast like a short saga, and Hollis is gentle and sceptical. The same stones read very differently in each voice, so it is worth trying more than one.

Is my question stored?

Your question is sent only to write the reflection. Please leave out names, contact details and other private information.

Does it replace a live advisor?
