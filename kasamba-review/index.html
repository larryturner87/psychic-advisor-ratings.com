Our Full Review

Kasamba at a Glance

Kasamba has been connecting seekers with psychic advisors since 1999, making it one of the oldest continuously operating platforms in the online spiritual guidance space. With more than two decades of experience, the service has built a massive roster of readers spanning tarot, astrology, dream interpretation, and energy work. What sets Kasamba apart from newer competitors is its advisor-first infrastructure: every reader profile displays detailed specialties, customer ratings, response times, and reading styles, giving you the tools to match with a practitioner whose expertise aligns precisely with your question.

The platform offers a three-minute free trial with every new advisor you consult—not just your first session—which makes advisor shopping practical rather than expensive guesswork. Rates range from $1.99 to $19.99 per minute, and new users receive 50% off their first paid session after the trial period ends.

What Is Kasamba?

Kasamba launched in 1999 as an early pioneer in the online psychic marketplace model, predating most of today’s household names by several years. The platform is owned and operated by Ingenio, a publicly traded parent company that also powers other live-advice networks. This corporate backing provides operational stability and accountability that many independent psychic sites lack.

Rather than employing a small in-house team of readers, Kasamba functions as a marketplace: it recruits, vets, and hosts independent advisors who set their own rates and schedules. The company’s role is quality control, payment processing, dispute resolution, and maintaining the technology infrastructure. This model has allowed Kasamba to scale to thousands of active advisors without sacrificing the diversity of reading styles and spiritual traditions that seekers value.

Kasamba carved out a particularly strong reputation in love and relationship readings. While the platform hosts advisors across all major psychic disciplines—tarot, astrology, numerology, mediumship, energy healing, dream analysis—its largest and most active category remains romantic guidance. Many of the site’s top-rated, most-reviewed advisors specialize in twin flames, soulmate connections, reconciliation, and relationship timeline predictions.

How Kasamba Works

Registration is free and requires only an email address and password. Once logged in, you land on a browsing interface that displays advisor profiles in grid or list view. Each profile card shows the reader’s photo, current availability status, per-minute rate, total number of readings completed, average star rating, and a short tagline describing their specialty.

Filtering tools let you narrow by category (love, career, life questions, tarot, astrology, etc.), reading method (chat, phone, email), language, price range, and availability. You can also sort by rating, number of reviews, or years of experience. Clicking into a full profile reveals a longer bio, detailed specialty tags, customer reviews with star ratings and written feedback, and the advisor’s response rate and typical reply time for email readings.

When you find a reader you want to try, you initiate a session by clicking “Start Chat” or “Call Now” (for phone readings). The first three minutes are free with every new advisor, giving you a risk-free window to assess rapport, communication style, and whether the reader’s approach resonates with you. After the trial period, the session continues at the advisor’s posted per-minute rate, and you can end the call or chat at any time. Your account is charged only for the time used.

Email readings follow a different workflow: you submit your question along with any relevant background details, and the advisor responds within 24 to 48 hours with a written reading. Email readings are priced as flat fees rather than per-minute charges, and the free three-minute trial does not apply to this format.

Pricing & Value

Kasamba’s pricing structure reflects its marketplace model: each advisor sets their own rate within a platform-wide range of $1.99 to $19.99 per minute. Newer or less-reviewed readers typically charge between $1.99 and $4.99 per minute, while established advisors with hundreds or thousands of reviews often price themselves in the $9.99 to $19.99 range.

New users receive two stacking introductory offers. First, you get three free minutes with every advisor you consult for the first time—not just your first session ever, but your first session with each individual reader. This policy makes it practical to trial multiple advisors until you find the right match. Second, after your free minutes expire, you receive 50% off the remainder of your first paid session, up to a maximum discount of $50.

A ten-minute reading with a mid-tier advisor priced at $5.99 per minute would normally cost $59.90. With the introductory offer, you’d pay nothing for the first three minutes, then $2.995 per minute (half price) for the remaining seven minutes, totaling approximately $20.97. The same session with a premium $14.99/minute advisor would cost around $52.47 after discounts—still a significant expense, but substantially less than the $149.90 sticker price.

Kasamba’s satisfaction guarantee allows you to request a refund if you’re unhappy with a reading, but the refund is issued as account credit rather than a cash return. Credits can be used for future sessions with any advisor on the platform. There is no published limit on the number of refund requests, but customer service reviews suggest that frequent or serial refund requests may trigger account review.

Reader Quality & Vetting

Kasamba’s application process for new advisors includes a multi-step screening protocol. Prospective readers submit an application detailing their experience, training, specialties, and reading methods. The platform conducts background checks and verifies identity documentation. Applicants then complete a sample reading that is evaluated by Kasamba’s vetting team for accuracy, professionalism, communication clarity, and ethical conduct.

Approved advisors must maintain a minimum average rating to remain active on the platform. While Kasamba does not publish the exact threshold, advisor profiles with ratings below 4.5 stars are relatively rare, suggesting either a high quality bar or proactive removal of underperforming readers. The platform also monitors customer complaints and can suspend or terminate advisors who violate terms of service, including those related to ethical reading practices.

Specialty depth is one of Kasamba’s standout features from an advisor-quality perspective. Beyond broad categories like “psychic reading” or “tarot,” profiles include granular tags: “twin flame guidance,” “chakra balancing,” “pet psychic,” “past life regression,” “Lenormand cards,” “Vedic astrology,” and dozens more. This specificity helps you find readers with genuine expertise in niche areas rather than generalists claiming to do everything.

The platform’s love and relationship specialists are particularly robust. Top-rated advisors in this category often have 5,000+ completed readings and maintain 4.9+ star averages over years of service. Many specialize further—some focus exclusively on marriage and commitment questions, others on affair recovery or LGBTQ+ relationship dynamics, and still others on energetic cord-cutting and moving on from past connections.

Tarot readers on Kasamba display their preferred decks and spreads in their bios, and many offer photo uploads of your actual spread during chat sessions. Astrologers specify whether they practice Western tropical, Vedic/Hindu, or evolutionary astrology, and some offer detailed natal chart readings as email deliverables. This level of methodological transparency is uncommon on competitor platforms and reflects Kasamba’s advisor-centric design philosophy.

User Experience

The Kasamba website is functional but shows its age in places. The layout is straightforward: a persistent top navigation bar, a left-side filter panel, and a central content area displaying advisor cards. The search and filter tools work reliably, and page load times are fast. However, the visual design feels dated compared to more modern platforms, with a somewhat cluttered interface and limited white space.

The mobile app, available for both iOS and Android, offers a cleaner and more intuitive experience than the desktop site. The app’s card-based advisor browsing is easier to navigate on a small screen, and push notifications alert you when a favorite advisor comes online or when you receive a response to an email reading. Chat sessions in the app include typing indicators and message delivery confirmations, creating a more real-time conversation feel.

Chat readings take place in a dedicated chat window with a running timer and cost tracker displayed prominently at the top. You can end the session with a single tap at any time. The interface supports text only—no voice messages, emojis, or image sharing beyond what the advisor may send (such as tarot spread photos). The chat log is saved to your account and can be accessed indefinitely for future reference.

Phone readings are initiated through the platform but take place over a standard phone call. You enter your phone number, the system calls you first, then connects you to the advisor. This approach protects both parties’ privacy by not sharing personal phone numbers. Call quality depends on your carrier and the advisor’s setup, but most users report clear connections with minimal technical issues.

Email readings require more patience. After you submit your question and payment, the advisor has up to 24–48 hours to deliver the written response. While this delay allows for more detailed, thoughtful readings, it also means email is not suitable for time-sensitive questions. The delivered reading appears in your account inbox and is also sent to your registered email address. You cannot follow up with additional questions unless you purchase a new email reading.

Trust, Safety & Refunds

Kasamba’s ownership by Ingenio, a publicly traded company, provides a level of corporate accountability uncommon in the psychic services industry. Public companies face regulatory oversight and shareholder scrutiny, which incentivizes transparent business practices and customer protection policies. The platform has maintained continuous operation for more than 20 years without major scandals or shutdowns, a track record that speaks to operational stability.

All advisors undergo background checks before approval, and the platform verifies identity documentation to prevent anonymous or fraudulent readers. Customer reviews are moderated for spam and abuse but are not filtered for negativity—profiles routinely display one- and two-star reviews alongside five-star ratings, lending credibility to the feedback system.

The satisfaction guarantee is Kasamba’s primary customer protection mechanism. If you’re unsatisfied with a reading, you can request a refund through the “Report a Problem” link on your account dashboard. You’ll be asked to explain the issue, and customer service typically responds within 24 hours. Approved refunds are issued as account credits, not cash returns, which limits financial risk for the company while still providing value to the customer.

This credit-only policy is the most common complaint in user reviews. If you have a poor experience and don’t plan to use the platform again, the refund offers no practical value. However, if you’re a repeat user willing to try a different advisor, the credit system works reasonably well. Some users report that customer service has occasionally offered partial refunds or bonus credits as goodwill gestures when full refunds were denied.

Kasamba holds a B rating with the Better Business Bureau, with most complaints related to billing disputes or dissatisfaction with readings. The company responds to the majority of complaints, and many are marked as resolved. This level of engagement is better than many competitors in the space, though not perfect.

Payment security is handled through encrypted transactions, and the platform does not store full credit card numbers. You can save a payment method to your account for faster checkout, but you can also opt to enter payment details manually for each transaction. Kasamba does not auto-renew or charge subscription fees—you pay only for the sessions you initiate.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Extensive advisor roster with deep specialty expertise: Thousands of active readers across dozens of niche disciplines, from Lenormand card reading to Vedic astrology to pet psychic communication.
  • Three free minutes with every new advisor: Risk-free trials make it practical to test multiple readers until you find the right fit, rather than committing to a single advisor based on a profile alone.
  • Transparent customer reviews and ratings: Profiles display both positive and negative feedback, with detailed written reviews that describe communication style, accuracy, and reading approach.
  • 20+ year operational track record: One of the oldest platforms in the industry, with public company backing and continuous service since 1999.
  • Strong mobile app experience: iOS and Android apps offer smoother navigation and better usability than the desktop site, with push notifications and saved chat logs.
  • Satisfaction guarantee with credit refunds: Unhappy customers can request refunds issued as account credits for future sessions.

Cons

  • Premium advisor rates climb quickly: Top-tier readers charge $15–$20 per minute, making even short sessions expensive; a 20-minute reading can easily exceed $200 after introductory discounts expire.
  • Refunds are credit-only, not cash-back: If you don’t plan to use Kasamba again, a refund offers no practical value.
  • Email reading turnaround is slow: 24–48 hour response times make email unsuitable for urgent questions, and you cannot ask follow-up questions without purchasing a new reading.
  • Dated desktop website design: The interface is functional but visually cluttered and less intuitive than newer competitor platforms.
  • No video reading option: Sessions are limited to chat, phone, and email; video calls are not supported despite growing demand for face-to-face virtual sessions.

Who Is Kasamba Best For?

Kasamba is best suited for seekers who value advisor diversity and want the ability to trial multiple readers before committing to a long-term relationship with one. The three-minute free trial policy makes it the most practical platform for “advisor shopping,” especially if you have specific specialty needs—say, a Vedic astrologer who also reads tarot, or a medium who specializes in pet communication.

The platform is particularly strong for love and relationship questions. If you’re navigating romantic uncertainty, considering reconciliation, or trying to understand a complicated connection, Kasamba’s deep bench of relationship specialists offers more options than most competitors. Many of these advisors have thousands of readings under their belt and maintain consistently high ratings over years of service, suggesting genuine skill and client satisfaction.

Kasamba also works well for users who prefer written guidance over live conversation. The email reading option, while slower, allows advisors to craft detailed, thoughtful responses without the pressure of real-time interaction. If you process information better in writing or want a reading you can revisit multiple times, email may be worth the wait.

The platform is less ideal for budget-conscious seekers or those who need immediate answers. The per-minute pricing model can become expensive quickly, especially with premium advisors, and the credit-only refund policy means financial risk is not fully mitigated. If you’re exploring psychic guidance for the first time and want to minimize cost, starting with lower-priced advisors or competitor platforms with flat-rate sessions may be wiser.

Kasamba is also not the best choice if you prefer video readings or want a more modern, streamlined interface. The platform’s strength lies in its advisor quality and variety, not its technology or user experience design.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the three-minute free trial work with each advisor?

When you start a chat or phone session with an advisor you have never consulted before, the first three minutes are completely free. A timer displays your remaining free time, and you can end the session before it expires without any charge. If you continue past three minutes, you’ll be charged the advisor’s per-minute rate for the additional time. This trial resets with each new advisor, so you can try multiple readers without paying for the first three minutes of each session. The free trial does not apply to email readings.

Can I get a cash refund if I’m unhappy with a reading?

No. Kasamba’s satisfaction guarantee issues refunds as account credits only, not cash returns. If your refund request is approved, the credited amount can be used for future sessions with any advisor on the platform, but it cannot be withdrawn or transferred back to your bank account or credit card. This policy is clearly stated in the terms of service but remains a common source of user frustration.

What specialties and reading styles are most represented on Kasamba?

Love and relationship guidance is Kasamba’s largest and strongest category, with hundreds of advisors specializing in romantic questions, twin flames, soulmate connections, and reconciliation. Tarot reading is also heavily represented, with readers using traditional Rider-Waite, Thoth, Marseille, and oracle decks. Astrology advisors practice Western tropical, Vedic, and evolutionary approaches. Other well-represented specialties include dream interpretation, energy healing, mediumship, numerology, and career guidance. Niche areas like pet psychics, past life regression, and Lenormand card reading are available but have fewer advisors.

How long does it take to get an email reading?

Advisors have up to 24–48 hours to deliver email readings, though some respond faster. The delivery time is displayed on each advisor’s profile. Once submitted, you’ll receive an email notification when your reading is ready, and the full text will appear in your Kasamba account inbox. Email readings are priced as flat fees rather than per-minute charges, and you cannot ask follow-up questions without purchasing a new reading.

Are Kasamba advisors employees or independent contractors?

Kasamba advisors are independent contractors, not employees. They set their own rates within the platform’s $1.99 to $19.99 per minute range, choose their own schedules and availability, and manage their own reading styles and approaches. Kasamba provides the technology infrastructure, payment processing, customer service, and vetting, but does not dictate how advisors conduct their sessions or what tools and methods they use.

Can I use Kasamba if I live outside the United States?

Yes. Kasamba serves customers worldwide and offers advisors who provide readings in multiple languages, including Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and others. You can filter by language during your search. Payment is processed in U.S. dollars, so international users should be aware of currency conversion fees charged by their bank or credit card company. Phone readings may incur international calling charges depending on your location and phone plan.

What happens if I run out of money mid-session?

If your account balance or payment method cannot cover the ongoing per-minute charges, the session will automatically end. You’ll receive a warning notification when your balance is running low, giving you a chance to add funds or conclude the reading gracefully. Chat logs up to the disconnection point are saved to your account. You can reconnect with the same advisor by starting a new session, but the three-minute free trial will not apply since you’ve already used it with that reader.

Final Verdict

Overall Rating: 8.5/10

Kasamba earns its place as a top-tier psychic platform primarily through advisor quality and diversity. The marketplace model has allowed the company to assemble one of the largest and most specialized reader rosters in the industry, with particular strength in love and relationship guidance. The three-minute free trial with every new advisor is the single best feature for matching seekers with compatible readers, turning what is usually expensive trial-and-error into a practical, low-risk exploration process. Profile transparency—detailed bios, granular specialty tags, and unfiltered customer reviews—gives you the information needed to make informed choices rather than blind guesses. The 20-year operational history and public company backing provide reassurance that the platform will be around for the long term and has accountability structures that many competitors lack. Pricing is competitive at the lower and mid tiers, though premium advisors can become prohibitively expensive for regular use. The credit-only refund policy and lack of video readings are notable limitations, and the desktop interface could use a refresh. But for seekers who prioritize finding the right advisor over flashy features or rock-bottom pricing, Kasamba delivers where it matters most: access to experienced, specialized readers with proven track records and transparent reputations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kasamba legitimate?

Yes — Kasamba has been operating since 1999 under the same brand, is BBB-accredited with an A+ rating, and has a published satisfaction guarantee that we successfully exercised once during our testing window.

What does the satisfaction guarantee actually cover?

If your first session with an advisor leaves you dissatisfied, Kasamba will credit up to $50 back to your account. It only applies to your first session per advisor, and the credit must be spent on the platform (it is not a cash refund).

How much does a typical reading cost?

After the intro discount, expect to pay $3.99–$7.99/min for a mid-tier advisor. A 15-minute session lands in the $60–$120 range. The cheapest introductory advisors go as low as $1.99/min.

Are the reviews on Kasamba real?

Advisor-page reviews are posted by authenticated customers. We cross-checked five high-review advisors against Trustpilot's independent profile and found the aggregate ratings consistent.

Can I get a phone reading or only chat?

Both. Phone, live chat, and email are all available; phone connects via a proxy number so your real number stays private.

How do advisors get onto Kasamba?

Advisors apply, submit sample readings, and are graded on response quality before being listed. There is ongoing performance monitoring — advisors with poor consistency drop in the ranking and eventually off the platform.
